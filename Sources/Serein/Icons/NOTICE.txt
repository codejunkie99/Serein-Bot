Some arrows are rotated or mirrored adaptations already present in Zeron.
Serein renders these assets with the interface text color and does not edit the SVGs, with two
exceptions. globe.svg is Serein's own drawing (an outline, one meridian and the equator), in place
of Solar's earth, whose coastlines read as a check mark at sidebar size. And widget-add.svg is Serein's adaptation of Solar's widget.svg (CC BY 4.0), three of its
four squares with a plus drawn in place of the fourth.

tinted orange). In Settings › AI apps and the model picker, a mark whose company has no colour

(@lobehub/icons, COLOR_PRIMARY #000 with a white avatar mark). The SVG files are unchanged. The product names and marks remain their owners' trademarks; no affiliation
or endorsement is implied.
